# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory comments, and publishing another person's private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through the contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that do not align with this code, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.
